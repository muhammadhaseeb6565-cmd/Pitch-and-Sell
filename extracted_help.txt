Welcome to the Pitch & Sell Help Center.

Pitch & Sell combines social content, product discovery, shopping, selling, and promotional tools in one platform.

This Help Center explains how the main features work and provides answers to common questions.

1. GETTING STARTED

What is Pitch & Sell?

Pitch & Sell is a social-commerce platform where users can discover short-form content and products while sellers and brands can showcase products, create product content, manage orders, and promote their offerings.

How do I create an account?

Open Pitch & Sell and select the appropriate sign-up option.

Depending on the available registration flow, you may register as:

User / Buyer

Buyer and Seller / Shop / Brand Owner

You may be asked to provide:

First name;

Last name;

Email;

Mobile number;

Profile picture or brand logo.

You may also be able to continue with Google.

Can I use Google to sign in?

Yes. Pitch & Sell supports Google Sign-In.

Select Continue with Google and follow the authentication instructions.

28. COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING

The video disappears but audio keeps playing.

Try:

Exit and reopen the feed.

Check your internet connection.

Restart the application.

Update the app if an update is available.

If the problem continues, report the affected video/feed issue to support.

Promotion says “Upload video first” even though I uploaded one.

Confirm that:

The video upload completed successfully.

The video is visible in your seller content.

The app has been refreshed.

The video meets the promotion requirements.

If the error remains, contact support.

My order review does not move to the next item.

Refresh the review section.

If the reviewed order remains visible after successful submission, report the issue to support.

Chat does not let me start a conversation.

If chat initiation is supported for your account but no start-conversation option appears, report the issue to support.
